Privacy policy

Live light shows. No account, no ads, no analytics. Your audio is analysed on your phone.

01Who we are and what we collect

This policy covers the Lumen Android app (package com.abdelquddos.lumen), provided by the independent developer reachable at abdquddos@gmail.com.

Lumen has no account system, ads, analytics or integrated crash-reporting service. We do not collect your audio, presets or settings, operate a server receiving them, or sell personal data. Light-show analysis happens on your device.

“On-device” describes Lumen’s audio processing and local app storage. Google Play processes purchases, and Android may back up app storage under your device’s backup settings. These services are separate from Lumen’s developer.

02Audio is processed live, in memory

  • Demo beat: generates a built-in rhythm for the lights. It does not access the microphone or other apps’ playback.
  • Microphone: with your permission, reads live microphone samples to calculate loudness, frequency bands and beat timing for the light show. The visible preview may also react to the microphone while this source is selected and permission is granted.
  • Ringtones: when you choose a song with Android’s file picker, Lumen reads that one file on your device to show a waveform, preview the lights and create a ringtone from the part you select. See “Ringtone files” below.
  • Phone audio: uses Android’s MediaProjection consent and AudioPlaybackCapture to analyse eligible media or game playback from other apps on this device. Each new capture session needs system consent. Lumen does not capture screen images or video, even though Android may describe the consent as screen sharing.

Microphone and Phone audio samples are analysed in temporary memory buffers in real time. They are never recorded to a file, stored as audio recordings, or transmitted. Lumen does not transcribe speech, identify songs, or build listening histories.

If playback appears silent while music is active, Lumen may try Android’s output-mix Visualizer compatibility mode. This also analyses live waveform data only in memory. Other apps, protected content and device restrictions may prevent capture; compatibility mode is not guaranteed to work.

Only derived light intensity frames are sent locally to the phone’s Glyph system service through the official Glyph SDK. Raw audio is not sent to that service.

03Local settings, presets and Android backup

Lumen stores settings and saved presets in private on-device Android DataStore files. These include preset names, light-show styles and tuning, selected audio sources and tempo, appearance and brightness preferences, onboarding state, and a cached Pro entitlement flag. Audio samples are not part of these files.

Ringtone files. When you save a ringtone, Lumen writes a new audio file (Ogg/Opus) containing the part of the song you selected and its light track to your phone’s shared Ringtones/Lumen folder through Android’s MediaStore. Unlike settings, these files are in shared storage: Android, your sound settings and file-manager apps can see them, and they remain on your phone if you uninstall Lumen. They are never uploaded by Lumen. If you use Share, the file goes only to the app you choose.

Android backup is enabled (allowBackup=true). Lumen has no custom backup exclusions. Settings, presets and the cached entitlement flag are eligible for Android’s backup and device-transfer mechanisms. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android may copy this app data to your backup provider or a new device. We cannot access those backups.

Control backup and restore through Android’s system settings; availability and retention depend on your device and provider. Google’s handling of its backup services is covered by its privacy policy. Lumen has no separate developer-run cloud sync.

Read Google’s privacy policy

04Google Play purchases

Lumen Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase (lumen_pro_lifetime) processed by Google Play Billing. A Lumen account is not needed; Google Play uses your Google account for the purchase.

The app queries purchase status, receives purchase information including a purchase token, and acknowledges purchases with Google Play to unlock or restore Pro. It caches the entitlement flag locally for offline use. Lumen does not send purchase information to a developer server and does not receive your payment-card details.

Google processes payment and transaction information under its privacy policy. Google Play and Android services may communicate with Google independently of Lumen’s on-device audio processing.

Read Google’s privacy policy

05Permissions and system access

Microphone / audio access
RECORD_AUDIO lets Lumen read live microphone audio. Android also requires it for Phone audio playback capture. Permission alone does not create a saved recording.
Phone audio consent
MediaProjection is a system consent flow, not a separate manifest permission. It authorises the Phone audio session. Lumen uses it for audio playback capture only, without screen-image capture.
Notifications
POST_NOTIFICATIONS lets Lumen show the active-show notification and its Stop action, and notify you when a free Phone audio session ends. Android controls how foreground-service notices appear if you deny notifications.
Foreground services
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE support user-started light shows while another app is visible. The service type depends on the source. Android and the phone’s Glyph service may impose background restrictions.
Audio settings
MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS supports Android’s output-mix Visualizer compatibility mode for Phone audio.
Modify system settings
WRITE_SETTINGS is a special permission you can grant in Android’s settings so Lumen can set a ringtone it created as your default ringtone or notification sound when you ask. Lumen does not change other system settings. Without it, you can still pick the ringtone in Android’s sound settings.
Glyph lights
com.nothing.ketchum.permission.ENABLE allows the official Glyph SDK to communicate locally with the phone’s Glyph service and control the rear lights. Lumen checks for that service; it does not upload an inventory of installed apps.
Google Play Billing and network access
com.android.vending.BILLING, INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are included by the billing dependency for Google Play purchase operations. Lumen does not use them to upload audio, presets or settings.
Internal AndroidX receiver protection
com.abdelquddos.lumen.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION is an automatically added signature permission used to protect internal broadcast receivers. It does not give access to personal data.

06Your control and deletion

Use Stop in Lumen or the ongoing notification to end an active show. You can revoke microphone permission in Android’s app settings and end Phone audio sharing using Android’s capture controls. To avoid microphone use in the visible preview, select Demo beat or revoke audio permission.

You can delete saved presets and ringtones in the app. Ringtone files can also be deleted with a file manager; uninstalling Lumen does not delete them. Clearing Lumen’s app storage or uninstalling removes its local settings and presets. System backups may remain under the backup provider’s retention rules or restore app data later; manage those copies in system or provider settings. Clearing app data does not cancel your Google Play purchase. Restore Pro with the purchasing Google account.

We hold no app account or server copy of your audio, settings or presets to delete. If you voluntarily email support, we receive the email address and information you send and use them to respond. Do not send audio or payment-card details. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to any legal retention obligations.

07Children

Lumen is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support correspondence, contact us so we can address it.

08Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if Lumen’s privacy practices change. Review this policy when updating the app.
